About This Project

This website offers a student-friendly mental wellness screening experience focused on awareness, reflection, and healthy support habits.

The assessment is designed to help senior high school students better understand their stress patterns and access practical coping strategies in a calm, supportive environment.

It includes confidential PHQ-9 and GAD-7 screening results, general next-step guidance, daily wellness tips, and help-seeking resources.

    Stress Management

    Identify academic triggers, practice time chunking, and utilize the Pomodoro technique to break large requirements down into manageable 25-minute sprints.

    Healthy Sleep Hygiene

    Aim for 8 hours of quality rest. Power down electronic screens at least 45 minutes before bedtime to support natural melatonin production.

    Physical Exercise

    Engage in at least 30 minutes of moderate activity like walking, stretching, or sports to release endorphins and reduce systemic stress.

    Healthy Nutrition & Hydration

    Maintain stable blood sugar levels by eating balanced meals and drinking sufficient water throughout your school day.

    Mindfulness & Meditation

    Spend 5 minutes daily focusing entirely on breath awareness to ground your thoughts and center your mental attention.

    Deep Breathing Techniques

    Try Box Breathing: Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, and hold empty for 4 seconds to lower heart rate.

    Time Management

    Use planners or digital calendars to schedule school requirements, study sessions, and intentional personal relaxation time.

    Positive Affirmations

    Replace self-critical loops with encouraging perspectives: 'I am learning at my own pace, and my worth is not defined solely by grades.'

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Everything you need to know about our mental health awareness platform

      What is Mental Well-being?

      Mental well-being is a state of psychological wellness where an individual realizes their own abilities, can cope with normal stresses of student life, can work productively, and contributes to their community.

      How does the assessment work?

      The assessment analyzes Likert-scale questionnaire responses across stress, sleep, motivation, socialization, and emotional regulation parameters to categorize current mental health balance.

      Is my information confidential?

      Yes, absolutely. All responses and scores are processed locally within your browser session or secure local storage. No personal diagnostic profiles are transmitted without consent.

      Does this website diagnose mental illness?

      No. This web portal is built strictly for educational self-awareness, screening orientation, and wellness tips. It is not a clinical instrument and does not replace certified psychological evaluations.

      How can I seek professional help?

      Students experiencing severe stress or mental distress should reach out directly to their school guidance counselor, campus health clinic, or licensed mental health practitioners.

      Disclaimer: This website is designed only for educational purposes and mental well-being awareness. It does not diagnose mental illnesses and should not replace licensed mental health professionals. If you are experiencing severe emotional distress, please seek assistance from your school's guidance counselor or a qualified mental health professional.
