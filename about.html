About This Project

This website helps senior high school students reflect on their mental well-being and discover supportive habits.

The assessment is designed to encourage self-awareness and provide simple guidance for stress management, rest, support, and healthy routines.

It was built as an educational awareness tool for students who want to better understand their emotions, habits, and wellbeing patterns in a calm and private way.

Each section of the website connects with the others: the assessment gives insight, the resources offer support, and the tracker helps monitor mood over time.

This website should be used as a supportive tool, not a medical diagnosis. If stress becomes overwhelming, talking with a trusted adult, school counselor, or healthcare professional is important.
